&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'm a system engineer and researcher working across computer security, embedded systems, RF, machine learning, bioinformatics and data-driven astrophysics. Over the last few years, most of my work has moved toward training models, integrating LLMs into practical systems, and applying machine learning to scientific data. I am especially interested in exoplanet detection, SETI, technosignatures, biosignatures and exobiology.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I currently work at the SETI Institute, applying machine learning to radio signals in the search for non-human technosignatures. In parallel, I have been exploring bioinformatics problems related to biosignals and exobiology, trying to connect computation, signal processing and life-detection questions in a more practical way.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before that, I spent decades working with backend, infrastructure, security engineering and embedded systems across areas such as power, payments, gaming and healthcare. That gave me a practical background in security, fault tolerance, real-time systems, automation, architecture, reverse engineering, industrial communication protocols and hardware/software integration. I'm also a licensed radio amateur (**PU7OLV**), and RF is still one of the things that keeps pulling me back.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I write mostly in `Python`, `C`, `C++`, `Go`, `Erlang`, `Clojure`, `Ruby` and `assembly(z80, x86, arm64)`, depending on what the problem needs. I like working close to the hardware, but I also enjoy building the data pipelines, tools and models that make raw signals useful.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I love coffee, it's my addiction. I've been traveling around the world working from coffeeshops.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I have 3 crazy dogs that were rescued and they are always close by.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I love classical computers and tech from the 1980's and 1990's, especially the Z80 and m68k based ones, to enhance and modernize. I've been writing games and general software for Sega Genesis, MSX, C64 and NES. It's a wonderful experience.
