01 / the operator ☉

I'm a researcher, systems engineer, signal analyst, hacker and chaos magician. This page is a facet of hypersigil that presents me to the world. Be my guest.

I work at the SETI Institute using machine learning to search for extraterrestrial technosignatures. I train models, work with radio and astrophysical data, and build the tools that help us look for intelligence beyond Earth. I listen and I teach computers to learn. I'm also a specialist in offgrid machines and it's quicks, reliability and its life cycle, like robots and satellites. I forge entities.

∴ machines, signals, symbols. sometimes all three at once.

03 / the ground ♁

Not everything here is cosmic horror. There is also coffee.

  • Old silicon. Z80 and m68k machines. Writing games and software for the Sega Genesis, MSX, C64, and NES. Giving old computers new things to do.
  • Radio. Licensed amateur PU7OLV. Antennas, RF, signal processing; another way of reaching beyond the room.
  • Many languages. Python, C, C++, Go, Erlang, Clojure, Ruby, and assembly: Z80, x86, ARM64. Whatever the machine needs.
  • The important stuff. Three rescued dogs, far too much coffee, and working from coffeeshops around the world.
